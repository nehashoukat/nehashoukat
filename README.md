### Research Background

My MSc Economics research examined how rainfall shocks affect household welfare in Pakistan and whether market access and economic integration can mitigate their effects.

The research combined household survey data with climate and spatial datasets, using econometric analysis alongside measures of rainfall shocks, travel time to markets and economic activity derived from satellite night-time lights.

I also worked as a Research Analyst at the University of British Columbia, developing Python and R geospatial pipelines using ERA5-Land climate data and administrative shapefiles, alongside Stata and R workflows for multi-year household and labour-force survey data.
